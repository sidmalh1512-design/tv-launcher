Avatar Q Launcher

Your pixel avatar on a 3×2 TV home screen. Use the arrows (or your keyboard) to move between tiles.

Moves

Left / right
Crouch, arms up, tucked front flip around the hips, open out, bent-knee landing.
Up
Deep crouch, spring with arms reaching, land on the higher tile.
Down
Small hop off the edge, fall with arms out for balance, deep landing crouch.
Open
Crouch-hop on the spot, then the app launches.
Idle
Two-frame breathing and a blink every few seconds.
